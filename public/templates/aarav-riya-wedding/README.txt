WEDDING WEBSITE TEMPLATE

Files:
- index.html: page structure and image links
- style.css: responsive styling and animations
- script.js: countdown, scroll reveal, mobile menu, demo RSVP

To preview:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. Internet is required for Google Fonts and the remote Unsplash images.

Customize:
- Replace Aarav & Riya, event names, dates, venue and address in index.html.
- Update the countdown date in script.js.
- Replace image URLs with your own wedding photos.
- Replace the Google Maps search link with your exact venue map URL.
- The RSVP form is demo-only; connect it to a backend or form service to store submissions.
