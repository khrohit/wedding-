WIX-INSPIRED WEDDING INVITATION — 3D EDITION

Includes:
- Animated 3D gold wedding rings in the hero (Three.js)
- Floating ambient particles and subtle pointer parallax
- Existing scroll reveal, countdown, responsive menu and RSVP demo
- Responsive layout and reduced-motion fallback

Preview:
Open index.html in a browser with internet access. Three.js is loaded from cdnjs, and photos/fonts are loaded from Unsplash/Google Fonts.

Customize:
- Edit couple names, date, events and venue in index.html.
- Change the countdown date in script.js.
- Replace image URLs with your own wedding photographs.
- Replace the Maps search URL with the exact venue share link.
- RSVP is a front-end demo; connect it to your backend/API to save responses.

Note: 3D rings require WebGL and internet access for the Three.js CDN.
