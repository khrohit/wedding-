PASTORAL WEDDING INVITATION — 3D SCROLL EDITION

An original pastoral/countryside-inspired wedding invitation design with botanical styling.

Features:
- Full-screen wedding photo hero with floating 3D petals and leaves (Three.js).
- Scroll reveal transitions and top progress indicator.
- Story and venue photo parallax.
- Countdown timer.
- Editorial event cards for Mehendi, Sangeet and Wedding.
- Gallery, venue map link and RSVP demo.
- Responsive mobile navigation and reduced-motion fallback.

Preview:
Open index.html in a modern browser. Internet access is needed for Three.js CDN, Google Fonts and remote Unsplash photos.

Customize:
- Names, wedding date, event information, venue and copy in index.html.
- Countdown date in script.js.
- Replace image URLs with your own photos.
- Replace the Google Maps search URL with your venue's exact map share link.
- RSVP form is front-end demo only; connect your backend/API or form provider to store responses.

Files: index.html, style.css, script.js, README.txt
